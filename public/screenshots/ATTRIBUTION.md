### Sourced from DistroWatch

These are the desktop screenshots DistroWatch publishes on each distribution's
table page. The originals are mostly 2560x1600; they are stored here at 1400px
wide (twice the card's 672px, so a HiDPI screen is still covered) with a light
unsharp pass to recover the edges the downscale eats, and encoded as webp q92.
Three sources were already smaller than that and are kept at their native size:
`tinycore` (1024), `dsl` (1280) and `libreelec` (1366) - they are never upscaled.

Every image is cropped to the region carrying the interface, found by edge
energy - wallpaper is smooth, panels and menus are not. This is the step that
makes a taskbar readable, and storing the image larger is not: the card is only
672px wide, so an uncropped 2560px desktop renders its 11px menu text at under
4px no matter how good the encode. It is also why the low-resolution shots in
the table above were left alone; they were already among the sharpest here.

Five were swapped for a different shot from the same distribution's DistroWatch
gallery, because the one on the table page was a bare desktop with no interface
in frame: `aerynos`, `alpine`, `chimera`, `mocaccino` and `postmarketos`.

`bodhi`, `pureos`, `rhel`, `secureblue` and `solus` are still bare desktops.
Their galleries do have shots full of interface, but the newest of those is from
2017 and shows a desktop that no longer resembles the current release, so the
recent-but-empty screenshot was kept instead.

DistroWatch hosts these images; it does not claim to own them, and it records
no per-image licence. Nearly
all show free-software desktops, and this site is a joke, so they are used here
with a link back to the source rather than a licence tag. If a project objects to
its screenshot appearing here, remove the file and drop the row.

`anduinos.webp` and `pikaos.webp` were taken from the same place in an earlier pass.
